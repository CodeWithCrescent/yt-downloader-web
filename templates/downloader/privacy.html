A. Information we collect

How we collect information. We may collect information automatically when you browse or use our Services, including technical data sent by your browser or device (for example, IP address, request time, requested URL, referrer, browser type, and similar HTTP headers). We may use cookies or similar technologies where necessary for security, preferences, or analytics. We do not intentionally collect “special categories” of personal data (such as health, religion, or biometric data) through GenDown.

Why technical data is collected. This information helps us deliver pages correctly, protect against abuse, understand aggregate usage, tune performance, and improve the Services. Unless stated otherwise, server logs and similar data are processed for operational and security purposes.

Sessions and functionality. We may use session technologies as needed for core features (for example, processing requests you initiate or protecting forms). If we display advertisements or use analytics partners in the future, those providers may set their own cookies or identifiers according to their policies.

Sharing links. If you use sharing features, we may generate URLs that contain an anonymous tracking parameter so we can measure how often shared links are opened in aggregate. That parameter is not intended to identify you personally.

No registration requirement. GenDown is designed to work without creating an account. If we ever offer optional accounts or newsletters, we will explain what we collect at that time.

Clipboard. Some browsers allow pasting a URL from your clipboard when you choose “paste.” That action occurs on your device under your control; we do not receive clipboard contents unless your browser sends them as part of a request you make.

Children. The Services are not directed at children under the age where parental consent is required in your jurisdiction for online services. If you believe we have inadvertently collected personal information from a child, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

B. Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device to keep sessions stable, remember preferences, or help us understand how the site is used. We may use essential cookies required for security or basic operation. Non-essential cookies (such as for analytics or advertising) may be used where permitted and, where required by law, only after appropriate consent.

Third-party advertisers or analytics providers (if present) may use cookies or similar technologies under their own policies; we do not control those technologies. You can adjust cookie preferences in your browser. Blocking or deleting cookies may affect how certain features work.

Useful browser guides include documentation from Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Microsoft Edge / Internet Explorer.

Do Not Track (DNT). There is no uniform industry standard for DNT. We do not guarantee specific responses to DNT signals; third-party embeds may behave independently.

Third-party sites. Our site may link to other websites. Their privacy practices are their own; please review their policies.
